Wind

Tap the hour the wind comes from. 12 is straight at you, 3 is from your right, 9 is from your left.

Shot

Range card

Up clicks are positive, down clicks negative. The wind letter is the way to dial. Wind, angle and direction settings from the HUD apply to every row. The highlighted row is the HUD distance.

Profile

One profile per shooter. Each profile has its own rifles.

Deleting a profile also deletes its rifles.

Rifles

Rifles saved under the selected profile.

Each rifle keeps its own load, scope, weather and location. Everything saves on this device.

Cartridge and bullet

Picking a cartridge fills in a typical G1 factory load. Changing the grain rescales the coefficient to match. Hornady lists both a G1 and a G7 value for its bullets, so pick the drag model that matches the number you enter.

Leave the change at 0 to use the same velocity in every temperature. Many loads gain 1 to 2 fps per degree.

Rifle and scope

Scope height is the distance from the center of the bore to the center of the scope tube.

Weather and location

Leave pressure blank to estimate it from altitude. If you enter it, use station pressure, which is what a barometer or phone sensor reads, not the sea-level pressure from weather reports.

Coriolis effect needs your latitude and the shooting direction from the HUD. It is small, about 1 inch at 500 yards, and grows quickly at long range.

True to a real impact

Enter how many MOA up you needed to hit at one distance, from a real shot or from another app. Scope Dope adjusts the coefficient or the muzzle velocity to match, then fills in the rest of the card.

Back up and restore

Your rifles are stored only in this browser. Copy a backup code and keep it somewhere safe. Restoring adds the rifles from a code. It never replaces what you already have.

Dial directions move your point of impact. Follow the arrows on your turrets. These are estimates from a point-mass model with G1 or G7 drag, plus spin drift, Coriolis effect when you enter a latitude, and wind from any clock position. They leave out aerodynamic jump and powder temperature unless you set a velocity change per degree. Fire a few rounds at known distances and true your velocity or coefficient until the card matches your groups. Calculation model: unverified estimates.
